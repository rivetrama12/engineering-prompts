# Prompt: Generate Die Cavity Design

Use this prompt to create the die insert sequence for the 8 mm collar.

```text
Act as a cold-forming die design engineer. Create a four-station die insert cavity design for an 8 mm steel lockbolt collar on a 14B nut former.

Final part requirements:
- OD target = Ø10.25 mm
- OD J max = Ø10.31 mm
- Length target = 8.06 mm
- Length K max = 8.13 mm
- Functional bore = Ø8.50 mm
- Material = low-carbon steel, 0.15%C max
- Raw wire from sketch = Ø11.80 mm

Final tooling requirements:
- Die Insert IV controls outside contour only.
- Final Die Insert IV cavity land = Ø10.25 × 8.06 mm.
- Punch IV controls final bore at Ø8.50 mm.
- KO Pin IV ejects only and must not size the bore.

Generate:
1. Die Insert I preform cavity.
2. Die Insert II backward extrusion cavity.
3. Die Insert III semi-finish cavity.
4. Die Insert IV final contour/sizing cavity.
5. Cavity diameters, land lengths, entry angles, radii, reliefs, and notes.
6. A manufacturing table in mm.
7. Warnings for lap/fold, underfill, overfill, and excessive punch load.
8. A final station check against OD and length limits.
```
