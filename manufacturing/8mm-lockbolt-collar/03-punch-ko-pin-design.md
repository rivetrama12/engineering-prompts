# Prompt: Generate Punch and KO Pin Design

Use this prompt to generate corrected punch and kick-out pin geometry for the collar tooling stack.

```text
Act as a cold-forming punch and ejector tooling engineer. Design Punches I–IV and Kick-Out Pins I–IV for an 8 mm steel lockbolt collar on a 14B nut former.

Final collar requirements:
- OD target = Ø10.25 mm
- OD J max = Ø10.31 mm
- Length target = 8.06 mm
- Length K max = 8.13 mm
- Functional bore = Ø8.50 mm
- Nominal pin = Ø8.00 mm

Punch requirements:
- Punch I: first upset / shallow preform.
- Punch II: rough bore / backward extrusion start.
- Punch III: semi-finish bore and wall control.
- Punch IV: final bore control at Ø8.50 mm.
- Punch IV smaller Ø7.80 / Ø7.75 nose is lead-relief only, not final through-bore sizing.

KO pin requirements:
- KO pins eject and support only.
- KO Pin IV face should be below final bore, about Ø8.40 mm.
- KO Pin IV must not resize or bell-mouth the bore.

Generate:
1. Punch I–IV geometry table.
2. KO Pin I–IV geometry table.
3. Working lands, reliefs, radii, shoulders, and face details.
4. Final station stack check: Die IV + Punch IV + KO Pin IV.
5. Manufacturing notes for polishing, radius blending, tool material, and coating.
6. Trial measurement checks after first sample.
```
